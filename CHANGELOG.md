# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-16)

- Pre-send check with fourteen coded findings and allow, hold or block outcomes.
- Call-back validation: contact on file before the change, not the one supplied with it; read-back; freshness; ordering.
- Cooling-off tiers by amount, dual approval excluding the initiator and the person who entered the destination.
- Look-alike sender domains, shared destinations across vendors, country changes, unchecksummed wallet addresses.
- ABA, IBAN and EIP-55 validation with an in-package Keccak-256.
- Hash-chained JSONL audit log with `verify-log`.
